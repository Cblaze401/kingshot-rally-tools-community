Map guide
BadlandsPlainsFertile LandRuinAlert zoneCore · no TCs

The amber shading and dashed border mark 0.5× terrain speed. Outside that border the factor is 1×, with no gradual slowdown. Dashed route sections use the same amber color.

Thin outlines mark the live tracker's zones. The Ruin zone outline extends one tile beyond the slow-terrain border on its north and east sides. Alert and Core mark separate areas; they do not add another slowdown.

Your TC covers four tiles in a 2 × 2 square. All four tiles must stay outside the hatched castle core. Other live obstacles and temporary Alert-zone restrictions are not shown.

Drag your TC or target to move it. Select a destination to see its route. Use Pan to move the map, the curved arrows to rotate by 45°, and Fit points to bring every destination into view.

Map positions mark movement centers. TC fields use the visible lower corner; its center is one tile higher on both axes. Target fields follow the chosen coordinate reference.

Keyboard: arrow keys move the map cursor; Enter places the point. On a movable footprint, arrows move that point. Hold Shift for ten-tile steps. Escape cancels a drag. Coordinate fields also work without dragging.

Bonuses and terrain

Keep ordinary, hunting and pet bonuses in their separate fields. Built-in scout and mercenary bonuses are included automatically.

Ruin terrain applies a 0.5× speed multiplier; outside terrain applies 1×. Each terrain segment is rounded up before the times are added. Turn on raw seconds to see the total before rounding.

In-flight 25% and 50% items reduce time remaining. Do not add them to your march speed bonus.
